The Wolf and the Jackal. On the Farm. A coloring storybook for kids!

Page 1

Winter came again. White snow fell everywhere, and the wind blew icy cold.

Page 2

Wolf was hungry again. He looked for food all day, but he found nothing. His tummy rumbled. Grrrumble!

Page 3

Then Wolf met Jackal. Jackal looked round and happy, and he was licking his lips.

Page 4

"Jackal," asked Wolf, "everyone is hungry in winter. Why are you never hungry?"
Jackal smiled a sneaky smile. "Tonight, I'll show you."

Page 5

That night, the moon was big and bright. Jackal led Wolf through the forest, softly, on tiptoe.

Page 6

They came to a farm. There was a red barn, a wooden fence, and a little henhouse.

Page 7

"Shh!" Jackal pointed down. Under the fence was a little hole. "I dug it myself!"

Page 8

There was another hole at the bottom of the henhouse. "Every night I crawl in here," Jackal whispered, "and take a chicken."

Page 9

"Can I do it too?" asked Wolf. Jackal thought for a moment, then nodded. "Yes! Let's do it together."

Page 10

Jackal slipped through easily. Wolf had to squeeze and squeeze… and he just fit!

Page 11

Inside, the chickens were fast asleep. Wolf and Jackal had a big feast, then crept quietly back out.

Page 12

The next night, and the night after that, they came back again. Wolf's tummy grew rounder and rounder.

Page 13

Then came the last night. They crawled into the henhouse again.
Jackal ate just a little, and he kept one eye on the hole.

Page 14

But Wolf ate, and ate, and ate. His tummy grew as round as a ball.

Page 15

"Wolf, don't eat so much!" Jackal whispered. "We have to fit back through that hole!"
"Just one more bite," said Wolf. And one more… and one more…

Page 16

"Bawk!" A hen woke up. Then the whole henhouse squawked: "Bawk! Bawk! Bawk!"

Page 17

"Run!" said Jackal. His tummy was still small, so he slipped out of the hole in a flash.

Page 18

Wolf pushed his head out. Then his shoulders. Then… STUCK! His big tummy would not fit through.
"I'm stuck!" cried Wolf.

Page 19

A light came on in the house. The farmer stepped outside in his pyjamas, holding a slingshot.

Page 20

Ping! A little pebble hit Wolf right on the bottom. "Ouch!" Ping! "Ouch!"
Wolf wiggled and wiggled… and popped out of the hole!

Page 21

Wolf ran home through the snow. His bottom was sore and his tummy ached. Jackal was long gone.

Page 22

Wolf sat down and thought. "Taking things that aren't mine is wrong. And taking too much only brings trouble."

The lesson

Never steal. What belongs to someone else is not yours to take.
And don't take more than you need.
